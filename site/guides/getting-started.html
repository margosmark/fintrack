Getting started

Log in, try the demo, and read your dashboard in 2 minutes.

  1. Try the demo first

    On the login screen, press “Explore the demo”. It loads a fully seeded household — accounts, transactions, rules, investments — so you can click through every page before entering a cent of your own data. Nothing you do in the demo touches a real account.

  2. Read the dashboard

    The dashboard opens with Net worth (sum of all account balances; investment accounts use their latest synced prices), Month net (income minus spending for the period), and Spending insights below. The 1W / 1M / 3M / 6M / YTD / 1Y buttons — or the From/To dates — re-scope every number on the page.

  3. Move around

    The sidebar holds the main sections: Dashboard, Transactions, Imports, Investments, Reports, Taxes, Settings — plus Tools (Currencies, Tags, Rules, Budgets, Goals, Bills, Debts). The search box at the top finds transactions, accounts, and tags from anywhere.
