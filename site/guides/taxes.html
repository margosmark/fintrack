Taxes (Canada)

ACB-tracked capital gains and CRA tax projection.

  1. Gains use average cost (ACB)

    Canadian rules require the adjusted-cost-base method: buys raise your cost base per holding, sells realize gains against it. The Taxes page shows the gain per year and the ACB pool per holding in non-registered accounts — recomputed from your transactions, never hand-edited.

  2. Keep buys and sells accurate

    The pools rebuild from your non-registered trade history, so accurate buys, sells, and corporate actions are all it takes. If numbers look stale after edits, use Rebuild — the single sanctioned write path for cost pools.

  3. Project next year's bill

    Tax Projection estimates federal + provincial (ON, BC, AB) tax from employment, capital-gains, and dividend inputs with deductions — showing taxable income, tax amounts, and average/marginal rates for planning, not filing.
